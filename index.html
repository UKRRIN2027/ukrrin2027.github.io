Welcome to UKRRIN 2027

We are thrilled to announce that the University of Leeds will host the UKRRIN 2027 Student Conference. This event brings together brilliant student researchers across the UK rail research network to showcase innovation, safety, digital data, and sustainable transport solutions.

Call for Abstracts

Submissions are subject to a 2-page limit and must clearly articulate the research objectives, methodology details, and a discussion of results. A standard template will be provided for all delegates to use.
