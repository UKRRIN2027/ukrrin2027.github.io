Welcome to UKRRIN 2027

The UKRRIN Student Led Conference is the premier annual event for PhD students in the UK conducting research with applications in the railway industry. We are thrilled to announce that the 2027 edition will be hosted by the University of Leeds.

The conference brings together student researchers from across the UK rail research network, alongside members of the academic community and industry professionals, to discuss innovative solutions in areas such as safety, digital data and sustainable transport, and to help shape the future of the railway. To this end, we will feature keynote speakers from leading organisations and provide networking opportunities to facilitate collaboration across the railway sector.

Participants will have the chance to showcase their work in an interdisciplinary environment spanning fields such as engineering, computer science, economics and the social sciences. The conference encourages students to present their research through poster presentations and talks, providing a platform for emerging ideas that could shape the future of the rail industry.

Conference Themes

We welcome contributions across the following themes:

Easy to use for all

Customer experience and accessibility.

Freight friendly

Infrastructure, operations, sustainability, and access to freight.

Low emissions

Electrification, decarbonisation, and energy management.

Optimised train operations

Improving infrastructure, planning, timetabling, improving capacity, improving incident recovery, and maximising lower use lines.

Efficient and reliable assets

Accelerating railway development while minimising risks, testing and verifications, railway resilience and security, and safe and incident maintenance.

Economics and policy

Applied railway economics and appraisal implications, including economics and policy contributions designed to enable system optimisation and innovation through an understanding of value and incentives.
